Introducing the DFW Lean Leader Regional Accelerator

Develop Your Own Lean Leaders.

Chick-fil-A’s Lean vision, championed by Forrest, starts with a simple idea: build leaders who improve systems, so your team can focus on what matters most.

That’s exactly what the DFW Lean Leader Regional Accelerator is designed to do. Here’s how it works:

1

Two days of hands-on training in DFW

Learn Lean by doing it, side by side with other Dallas Fort Worth leaders.

2

Guided improvement projects

Put it to work in your own restaurant, with guidance every step of the way.

3

The Lean365 pathway

Keep growing your leaders through the Lean365 learning pathway.

What this means for your restaurant

✓  Leaders who spot and solve problems before they reach you
✓  Improvement that happens inside your own four walls
✓  More time for your team, your guests and your growth
